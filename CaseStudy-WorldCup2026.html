01Overview

Two routes to the same asset library

This study sets two production models side by side. The 2022 Betway campaign was built through a manual, photoshoot-led workflow. The 2026 Quantum campaign is built through an AI-led workflow on Weavy.

Until now, football campaigns produced through Podium followed a manual model — source a shoot, then apply kits, adapt players and build team variations by hand. The 2026 World Cup campaign is the first time we apply AI-led production to a library of this kind.

We use the 2022 output of 640 assets as the benchmark. The 2026 workflow is built to be demand-responsive — we generate assets against real teams, fixtures and campaign moments rather than producing everything upfront.

In summary

A faster way to the same output — and a smarter model behind it.

With an AI-led workflow, we produce a comparable 640-asset football library in around 18.17 hours, against 320 hours on the 2022 model. Including allocated source imagery and manual production, the 2022 toolkit carried a total relevant cost of roughly £43,333. The AI-led workflow brings that to about £2,270.83 — a saving of £41,062.17.

This is the first real iteration of AI-led production applied to a football library that has always relied on manual work through Podium. The result is not just a faster route to the same output. It is a more responsive, scalable and demand-led model.

Where 2022 produced a fixed library, the 2026 workflow creates assets in step with real campaign demand. Less wasted effort, more flexibility, and a more efficient model for the sports campaigns we build next.
