WHY RACING

Why a car game

The theme wasn't a creative whim—it came straight out of competitor research.

The mechanic works

Competitor research shows racing themes and refuel mechanics are proven engagement loops for sweeps players—the fuel gauge gives players a reason to keep tapping.

The market is moving

In UK igaming, Super Free Slots Games was already testing racing as an offer theme on social. We're meeting a validated trend, not guessing at one.

Built for both markets

One concept flexes across US sweepstakes and UK igaming—same game engine, same asset system, market-specific offers on top.

THE STACK

How we built it

AI ran through the whole pipeline—generation, production and publishing each had the right tool for the job.

STEP 1
GENERATE

Weavy

Every car, crash state, coin, bolt and background generated through a single Weavy flow—50 unique elements including the AI video background, all style-locked to one visual world.

STEP 2
REFINE

Photoshop

Production tweaks only—cutouts, plate branding and final polish. AI did the heavy lifting; Photoshop made it shippable.

STEP 3
SHIP

Bannerflow

Build and publish in one place. Four iterations went out in 15 minutes—test, learn and re-ship at a pace static workflows can't touch.

The full generation flow is documented in Weavy—every element traceable back to its prompt. Open the flow

THE EDGE

What this offers that other games haven't

Elements at AI speed

All 50 elements built with AI—roughly 3 hours of generation against the month a traditional pipeline needs, at a higher standard within the time.

Video, for the first time

The first of our games to run AI video in the background—a moving world behind the gameplay instead of a static plate, with the build kept light.

Multiple journeys

Left or right—every move branches the run. Multiple player journeys deepen immersion and give the game replay value a single-path unit can't offer.
